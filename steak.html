The Best steak Recipe




Description:

This is the best pizza recipe. It is an award winning recipe that is easy to make and tastes great.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pizza dough
  • 1 cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup pepperoni

Instructions:

  1. Knead dough
  2. Spread tomato sauce onto circular dough
  3. Add cheese on top of our tomato sauce
  4. Spread pepperoni on top of our cheese
  5. Put the pizza in the over at 375 degrees Fareinheit for 25 minutes
  6. Let the pizza cool for 10 minutes, then enjoy!
